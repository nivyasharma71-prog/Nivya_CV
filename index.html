Design Projects

Jaipur Architecture Collection

A jewellery series drawing from the geometric jalis, arched corridors, and decorative stonework of Jaipur's historic monuments. Motifs abstracted into contemporary silhouettes using CorelDRAW and manual gouache rendering.

Northern Lights Collection

An exploration of borealis phenomena translated into jewellery form — fluid curves, iridescent material pairings, and sweeping enamel gradients capturing the movement of auroral ribbons across the sky.

Nature's Geometry Collection

Fibonacci spirals, crystalline lattices, and organic cellular forms rendered as precise jewellery pieces. A study in the mathematical elegance found in the natural world, executed through Rhino 3D modelling and technical illustration.
